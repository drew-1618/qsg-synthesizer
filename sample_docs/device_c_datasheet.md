# Corvane AX9000 — Datasheet

## Overview

The Corvane AX9000 is an enterprise Wi-Fi 7 gateway for multi-dwelling
units and small campuses.

## Key Features

- Multi-Link Operation (MLO) across 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz
- Dual 10G SFP+ uplinks with failover
- Built-in Zigbee and Thread radio for building IoT
- Cloud management with zero-touch onboarding

## Specifications

- Wi-Fi Standard: 802.11be (Wi-Fi 7), tri-band
- WAN Speed: Up to 10 Gbps (SFP+)
- Form Factor: Ceiling or wall mount, plenum rated
- Max Wireless Throughput: 19 Gbps combined
- Power Consumption: 28 W typical, 36 W max (PoE++)
- Operating Temperature: 0 to 45 °C
- Dimensions: 240 x 240 x 48 mm
- Weight: 1.4 kg
- Certifications: FCC, CE, UL 2043
